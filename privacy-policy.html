SANGAT Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

SANGAT is a battery monitoring and alert application. It speaks voice alerts when your phone starts charging, stops charging, is full, is low, or is overheating.

1. Information We Collect

SANGAT does not require you to create an account. SANGAT does not collect, store or transmit personal information to any server. The app does not request internet access.

2. Audio Recordings

If you create custom voice alerts, the recordings are stored only on your device, in SANGAT's private storage. SANGAT does not upload, share, sell or transfer your recordings to anyone. Recordings are not included in cloud backups.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: to show that battery alerts are active.
  • Microphone: only when you choose to record a custom voice alert.
  • Foreground service: to keep battery alerts active while the app is closed and alerts are switched on.
  • Wake lock: so an alert can finish playing while the screen is off.
  • Boot completed: only if you turn on "Start alerts after phone restart".

4. Data Sharing

SANGAT does not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties. SANGAT contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all custom recordings at any time from Settings inside the app. Uninstalling SANGAT removes all app data stored on your device.

6. Children

SANGAT does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
